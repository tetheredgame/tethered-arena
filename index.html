Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Tethered: Arena collects limited anonymous gameplay analytics to help improve the game.

This may include an anonymous player identifier, gameplay events, game mode, score, results, app version, and anonymous session information.

Tethered: Arena does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, or other information intended to directly identify you.

Analytics data is used to understand how the game is played, identify problems, and improve the game. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

For privacy questions or support, contact: tetheredgame@hotmail.com
